About WeatherWise

We don't just show you the weather. We tell you what to do with it.

What Is WeatherWise?

WeatherWise is a weather intelligence tool designed to answer the questions that standard weather apps never bother with. Knowing it's 72°F and partly cloudy is fine — but what you really want to know is: Should I go for a run? Will my laundry dry outside? Is today a bad hair day?

We take real-time weather data and run it through purpose-built scoring algorithms to give you clear, practical answers — presented in plain language, not meteorological jargon.

Our Mission

The mission of WeatherWise is simple: make weather data useful. Most weather websites and apps are built around data — temperature, humidity, UV index — but they leave the interpretation entirely up to you. We believe that's backwards.

Weather data is only valuable when it's actionable. WeatherWise bridges the gap between raw numbers and real decisions, so you can plan your day with confidence.

How Our Scores Work

Each decision score is calculated using a weighted combination of weather variables sourced in real time from WeatherAPI.com. Here's how we compute each one:

🏃

Running Score

Combines temperature (ideal: 50–65°F), humidity, UV index, air quality index (AQI), and rain probability. High heat, humidity, or poor air quality reduces the score significantly.

👗

Laundry Score

Weighs humidity (the primary factor), wind speed (wind accelerates drying), cloud cover, and chance of rain. A sunny, breezy, low-humidity day scores highest.

💇

Hair Frizz Risk

Driven primarily by relative humidity. Below 55%: low risk. 55–74%: moderate risk. 75% and above: high risk. A simple but highly accurate predictor of frizz days.

🌿

Outdoor Activity Score

A composite score that factors temperature, UV index, AQI (air quality), rain chance, and wind speed. Good for general outdoor planning — picnics, sports, yard work.

😴

Sleep Comfort Score

Based on tonight's forecast low temperature (ideal: 60–67°F) and current humidity. High overnight temperatures and humidity are the biggest sleep disruptors.

🚴

Bike Ride Score

Factors temperature, wind speed (wind above 20 mph is a major negative), rain probability, and UV exposure. Designed for recreational and commuter cyclists.

Data Source

WeatherWise is powered by WeatherAPI.com, a leading weather data provider offering real-time conditions, 7-day forecasts, UV index, air quality data, and astronomical data (sunrise/sunset) for cities worldwide.

All data is fetched fresh each time you search. We do not cache or store your search history, and we do not collect any personally identifiable information.

Important Disclaimer

WeatherWise scores are estimates intended to help with everyday decisions. They are not a substitute for professional advice, official weather warnings, or emergency guidance. Always check your local meteorological authority during severe weather events. See our full Disclaimer for details.
