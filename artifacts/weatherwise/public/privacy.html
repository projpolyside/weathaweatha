Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

At WeatherWise, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using WeatherWise, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

WeatherWise does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the service. The only information you actively provide is the city name you enter in the search field. This is used solely to retrieve weather data and is not stored or logged on our servers in any identifiable way.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit WeatherWise, we may automatically receive certain technical information, including:

  • Your IP address (used by our hosting provider for security and traffic analysis)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Date and time of your visit

This information is collected in aggregate and is used to understand how users interact with WeatherWise so we can improve the service. It is not used to identify you personally.

2. Cookies

WeatherWise uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and to serve relevant advertising. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the site to function correctly (e.g., session management, security).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the website (e.g., pages visited, time on site). We may use Google Analytics for this purpose.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to serve personalized advertisements based on your browsing behavior across the web. See Section 4 for more details.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the site or the relevance of advertisements shown.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the WeatherWise service
  • Improve and optimize the website experience
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Analyze usage trends to improve our scoring algorithms and content
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Google AdSense & Advertising

WeatherWise uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to WeatherWise and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Our AdSense publisher ID is: ca-pub-8736297902100969.

Google's privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Third-Party Services

WeatherAPI.com

Weather data is provided by WeatherAPI.com. When you search for a city, a request is sent to WeatherAPI.com on your behalf from our server. Your search query (city name) is transmitted to WeatherAPI.com's servers. Please review WeatherAPI.com's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

Hosting & Infrastructure

WeatherWise is hosted on cloud infrastructure. Our hosting provider may collect standard server logs including IP addresses for security and performance monitoring purposes.

6. Data Retention

We do not store personal data collected through the WeatherWise interface. City search queries are processed in real time and are not logged or retained. Any data collected automatically through server logs is retained only as long as necessary for security and operational purposes, typically no longer than 90 days.

7. Children's Privacy

WeatherWise is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your personal data for direct marketing.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via the Contact page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of WeatherWise after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
